YOUR TURN: OPEN YOUR DOOR!

Imagine that you can speak English fluently. Complete this sentence:

“IF I COULD SPEAK ENGLISH FLUENTLY,
I WOULD…”

Study abroad.

Travel around the world.

Get my dream job.

Make friends internationally.

Learn something new every day.

SHARE YOUR ANSWER ALOUD IN ENGLISH!

Activity time, about five minutes. Ask everyone to finish the sentence “If I could speak English fluently, I would…” Use the cards for ideas, but encourage personal answers. Invite three to five students to say their sentence aloud. Praise every attempt — the goal is to speak, not to be perfect. Grammar tip: after “If I could…” we use “I would + verb”.
