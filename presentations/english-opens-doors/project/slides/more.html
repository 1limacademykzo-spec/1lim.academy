WHY ENGLISH?

ENGLISH IS MORE
THAN A LANGUAGE

It is a passport to opportunities.

It connects people.

It creates opportunities.

It expands our world.

“One language.
A thousand possibilities.”

Ask the room: what is English for you? For many people it is just a school subject. But English is a bridge between cultures. It connects people, creates opportunities and expands our world. Think of it as a passport, or a golden key that unlocks the map.
