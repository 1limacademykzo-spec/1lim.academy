5. SELF-DEVELOPMENT —
GROW BEYOND LIMITS

English gives you access to an enormous world of knowledge.

Read books in English.

Watch films without relying entirely on subtitles.

Listen to podcasts.

Take online courses.

Discover new ideas and perspectives.

“Every new word is a new way
to understand the world.”

Door number five: self-development. Most of the world’s online courses, podcasts, films and books are available in English. Every new word you learn gives you a new way to understand the world. Ask: what is one film, song or podcast in English you enjoy?
